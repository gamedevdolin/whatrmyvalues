Values define how you want to behave—not what you want to achieve.

They guide your actions, your choices, and the compromises you're willing to make.

There are no "right" or "wrong" values. We all have different priorities. The goal is to identify yours.

This exercise will help you discover your core values in three steps:

1. Browse a list of values and select any that resonate
2. Narrow your selections down to your top 5
3. Reflect on what you've discovered
